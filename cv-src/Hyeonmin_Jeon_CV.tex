\cvsection{Research Experience}
\entry{Financial Investment AI Lab, Yonsei University}{Seoul, Korea}{Graduate Research Assistant}{Mar 2025 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
\item Generative scenario models for portfolio risk: a conditional diffusion transformer that generates joint multi-asset return scenarios, evaluated by the VaR/ES reserves set on its scenarios rather than by sample realism.
\item Timing in the equity cross-section: forecasting complex wavelet scalograms so that the phase of price moves, and hence when each stock reprices, is preserved and used for cross-sectional ranking.
\item Learned priors for Bayesian factor selection: replacing the mixture-of-$g$ prior with a normalizing flow that keeps an explicit density, checked against the exact posterior over models (TV distance 0.016) and tuned by Bayesian optimization; compared with a density-free GAN prior.
\item Ranking and selection under input uncertainty: implemented and verified KN, feasibility determination, recycled observations and CRN, and studied how a fitted input model affects the probability-of-correct-selection guarantee.
\end{itemize}
